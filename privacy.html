Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority.

We keep things simple: we collect only what's necessary to make Chithi work, and we treat your data with respect.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address (for account creation, login, and password recovery)
  • Basic account-related data required to operate the platform
  • Messages sent through the platform (so they can be delivered, viewed, and managed)

We do not collect unnecessary personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide secure access to your messages
  • To operate core features (such as message delivery, status, and notifications if applicable)

3. Messages & Content

  • Messages may be sent anonymously or with identity
  • Private messages are not publicly visible
  • Users have full control to read, reply, react to, or delete messages
  • Senders may also delete messages they've sent

Once deleted, messages may be permanently removed from our active systems.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data
  • Your information is never shared with third parties except when required to operate or protect the platform

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies only to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Maintain secure sessions

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely risk-free. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account secure.

7. Your Control

You can:

  • Delete messages
  • Manage your account
  • Request account deletion (if available)

By using Chithi, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
