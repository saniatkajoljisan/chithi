Good to know

Does sending or receiving letters count as a referral?

No. A referral only counts once someone creates a full account and picks a username through your link — letters aren't part of it.

Can I refer myself, or refer the same person twice?

No. Each account can only ever be counted as one referral, and an account can't refer itself.

Do my unlocked rewards ever expire?

No. Once a stop is unlocked, it stays on your account for good — you can keep switching between every theme you've unlocked at any time from your dashboard.

What happens after the 50-referral stop?

You've completed the current flight path! Keep an eye on your dashboard — new stops may be added as Chithi grows.
