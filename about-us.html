About Us

Connecting hearts, one letter at a time.

Welcome to Chithi—a simple, personal space designed for honest expression. Inspired by the timeless tradition of letter writing, Chithi brings back the depth and sincerity that often gets lost in fast, surface-level communication.

In a world where it's not always easy to say things out loud, Chithi gives your words a place to land.

How it works

Each user gets a unique, shareable link. Anyone with that link can send you a message—whether it's heartfelt, supportive, thoughtful, or even anonymous.

No feeds. No likes. No algorithms.
Just words that matter.

Our belief

We believe that:

  • Honest words build stronger connections
  • Anonymity, when used responsibly, can help people express what truly matters
  • Everyone deserves a safe, pressure-free space to share and receive emotions

Chithi is not a social network. It's a focused, minimal experience—built for meaningful exchanges, not noise.
